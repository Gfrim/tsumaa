Meet the Principal

The person
behind the studio.

/ 03
Kwabena Owusu

Founder & Principal Architect

Kwabena trained as an architect before spending six years running residential build sites, and started Atelier Solum on the belief that a building's design and its construction shouldn't be handled by two firms that never talk to each other. He leads every project personally, from the first site visit to the final handover.

“A drawing is a promise. My job is to make sure the building keeps it.”
