Renovation

Maple Hill Renovation

East Legon, Accra 2023 260 m²
Interior Consultancy
The Brief

A 1990s family home with good bones but a dark, compartmentalized interior. Our consultancy phase started with a feasibility review of which walls could safely come down, before the design work reopened the ground floor into one connected living space.

We kept the original brick fireplace as the anchor of the new layout — a deliberate contrast against the lighter, more contemporary finishes surrounding it.
