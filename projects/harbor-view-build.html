Harbor View Build

Ada Foah, Greater Accra 2023 510 m²
Exterior Landscaping Full Build
The Brief

Built on a sloped coastal plot, the brief called for a home that reads as part of the landscape rather than imposed on it — terraced gardens stepping down toward the water, and a material palette drawn from the surrounding stone.

We managed the full build ourselves, from earthworks and terracing through to the final planting, so the landscape and the architecture were never treated as separate contracts.
