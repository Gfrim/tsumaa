Design + Build

Courtyard Retreat

Aburi, Eastern Region 2022 190 m²
Landscaping Interior Consultancy
The Brief

A small hillside plot with an existing structure the owners wanted to keep. Our consultancy work focused on how much could be preserved versus rebuilt, landing on a scheme that wraps the retained structure around a new central courtyard.

Every interior room opens onto the courtyard, letting the landscaping do double duty as both garden and hallway — the smallest project in scope on this page, but one of the most tightly resolved.
