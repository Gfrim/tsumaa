Riverside Residence

Kpong, Eastern Region 2024 420 m²
Interior Exterior Full Build
The Brief

The clients wanted a home that opened fully onto the river frontage without sacrificing privacy from the road side — a house of two faces. We resolved this with a near-solid street elevation and a fully glazed rear, tying interior and exterior design into a single decision from the earliest sketches.

Construction ran across fourteen months with our own crews on site throughout, allowing design refinements to happen in real time as the building went up rather than being locked in on paper months in advance.
